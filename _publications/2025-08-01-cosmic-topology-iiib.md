summary: >-
  Computes, for the first time, how primordial gravitational waves (tensor perturbations) would carry the imprint of a finite, flat universe into the CMB.
detail: >-
  Derives spin-2 Laplacian eigenmodes and the corresponding CMB correlation matrices for orientable compact Euclidean manifolds. Over the multipole range computed, the topological information in tensor-induced anisotropies (measured by the KL divergence) keeps growing with ℓ, rather than saturating as quickly as in the scalar case.
contribution: >-
  Led the analytical derivation of spin-2 eigenmodes and CMB covariance matrices, together with the numerical implementation and analysis of the results.
why: >-
  Suggests that future CMB polarization data could open new avenues for detecting cosmic topology.
