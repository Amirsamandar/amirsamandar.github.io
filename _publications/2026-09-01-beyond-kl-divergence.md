tags_line: "Likelihood-ratio inference · Information theory · Detection probability"
excerpt: 'The KL divergence is an expected information gain and cannot, by itself, give a detection probability on a single realization. This work derives the exact sampling distribution of the log-likelihood ratio between two zero-mean Gaussian models — a generalized non-central chi-squared — and uses it to convert expected information gain into a detection probability with ROC curves and calibrated interval coverage. Supplies the frequentist validation layer that learned ratio estimators currently lack, and connects to Bayesian experimental design. Model-agnostic; cosmic topology is the case study.'
